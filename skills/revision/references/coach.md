# Revision coach mode

For every reviewer comment:
1. Preserve the source comment.
2. Split multipart comments.
3. Identify the underlying concern.
4. Assess whether the concern is correct, partly correct, ambiguous, or contestable.
5. Identify the smallest adequate manuscript action.
6. Identify evidence or analysis needed.
7. Record where the change belongs.
8. Recommend an author posture: agree, partly agree, or disagree with evidence.

## Output
| ID | Reviewer concern | Underlying issue | Author decision needed | Manuscript action | Evidence needed | Location | Response strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add dependencies and a practical revision order.
