# Evidence-certainty mode

Use an outcome-level framework such as GRADE only when it fits the evidence-synthesis context.

Assess, as applicable:
- Risk of bias
- Inconsistency
- Indirectness
- Imprecision
- Publication bias
- Factors that may increase certainty under the chosen framework

Keep certainty separate from statistical significance. A precise estimate from weak evidence is not automatically high certainty.

## Output
| Outcome | Studies | Participants | Effect estimate | Certainty | Reasons |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

Add a short rationale for every downgrade or upgrade.
