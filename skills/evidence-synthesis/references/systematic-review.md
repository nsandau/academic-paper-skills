# Systematic review mode

## Workflow

1. Formulate the review question and eligibility criteria.
2. Define databases, registries, grey-literature sources, and search dates.
3. Record complete search strings and filters.
4. Deduplicate records.
5. Screen titles and abstracts against predeclared criteria.
6. Screen full text and record exclusion reasons.
7. Create a study-extraction form before synthesis.
8. Extract study characteristics, methods, outcomes, and data needed for synthesis.
9. Assess risk of bias with a design-appropriate tool.
10. Decide whether synthesis should be narrative, quantitative, or mixed.
11. Produce a transparent study-flow account consistent with PRISMA principles when applicable.

## Output
- Review question
- Protocol decisions
- Search strategy
- Eligibility criteria
- Screening flow
- Included study table
- Excluded full-text reasons
- Extraction schema
- Synthesis plan
- Deviations from protocol
- Review limitations

## Reporting guideline

Use the current applicable systematic-review reporting guideline and extension. Verify the official version rather than assuming a hard-coded PRISMA version is current. The `reporting-guidelines` skill can perform the item-level compliance audit.
