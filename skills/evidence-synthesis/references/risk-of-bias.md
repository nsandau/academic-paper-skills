# Risk-of-bias mode

Choose the assessment instrument from the study design rather than convenience. For example, RoB 2 is designed for randomized trials and ROBINS-I for non-randomized intervention studies. Other designs may require different validated tools.

For every domain:
- Answer the instrument's signaling questions from study evidence.
- Cite or point to the study text supporting the judgment.
- Distinguish missing reporting from demonstrated high risk.
- Follow the instrument's aggregation logic rather than inventing an average score.

## Output
- Study
- Tool used and why
- Domain-level judgments
- Evidence for each judgment
- Overall judgment
- Unclear or unavailable information
- Sensitivity implication for synthesis
