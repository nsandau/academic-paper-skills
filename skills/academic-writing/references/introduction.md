# Introduction mode

Use a CARS-like rhetorical check when appropriate:
1. Establish the research territory and why the topic matters.
2. Establish the problem, tension, limitation, or gap in current knowledge.
3. Occupy the niche by stating the study objective, RQ, approach, and contribution.

Check whether the introduction:
- Gives enough context without becoming a literature review
- Makes the problem concrete
- Represents prior work fairly
- States a defensible gap
- Connects the gap to the RQ
- Makes the contribution commensurate with the evidence
- Avoids promising results that the paper does not deliver

For title review, check informativeness, searchable concepts, unnecessary claims, population or design descriptors when useful, and venue conventions.
