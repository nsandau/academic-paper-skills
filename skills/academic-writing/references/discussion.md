# Discussion mode

Build or review the section around:
finding -> interpretation -> relation to prior literature -> plausible mechanism -> alternative interpretation -> implications -> boundary conditions -> limitations

Check that the Discussion:
- Does not merely repeat Results
- Distinguishes observed results from interpretation
- Addresses findings that complicate the preferred story
- Compares results with relevant prior evidence
- Does not claim mechanisms that were not tested
- Does not turn statistical significance into practical importance automatically
- Makes limitations specific to inference
- Ends with implications proportionate to the evidence
