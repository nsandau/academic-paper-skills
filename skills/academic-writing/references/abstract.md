# Abstract mode

A useful empirical abstract usually covers:
- Background or problem
- Purpose or question
- Method
- Main findings
- Implications or conclusion

Adapt to venue format and paper type. Use exact reported findings when they are central. Do not introduce claims, numbers, or conclusions absent from the manuscript.

Quality checks:
- Self-contained
- Within verified venue word limit when known
- No unnecessary background
- Methods identify the design and sample or corpus when important
- Findings are concrete
- Conclusion matches the evidence
- Keywords complement rather than simply repeat the title
